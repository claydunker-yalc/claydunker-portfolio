About

Hey, I'm Clay.

I'm a teacher and web developer from Monticello, IL. I spend my days in the classroom and my evenings building things for the web. I started offering web design services because I kept seeing great local businesses with no online presence — and I knew I could fix that.

The dashboards came from the same place. Once a business is online, the next problem is usually that its numbers are scattered across a CRM nobody wants to log into. So I build the one screen that puts them together.

Either way, you get real, hand-coded work — not a template you could've built yourself on Squarespace. And you get someone who actually cares whether it works.
